Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Barber Ji respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website and mobile application.

Depending on the features you use, Barber Ji may collect information such as:

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

3. Payments

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. Payment information may be handled by the applicable payment provider according to its own privacy policy and security practices.

Barber Ji does not intend to store sensitive payment credentials such as card PINs, CVVs or banking passwords on its own systems.

4. Location Information

Certain features may require location or address information, for example to help customers discover nearby service providers or to support home-service bookings.

Location access is used only when required for the relevant feature and subject to the permissions provided by the user.

5. Information Sharing

We may share necessary information with service providers or technology partners when required to provide a requested service, process a booking, process a payment, provide notifications, or comply with applicable law.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information for unrelated purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.
