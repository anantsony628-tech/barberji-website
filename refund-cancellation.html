1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations, refund eligibility and refund requests are handled on the Barber Ji platform.

Refund eligibility may depend on the booking status, payment method, reason for cancellation and the refund rules applicable to the booking.

Refunds are processed only when the booking is eligible under the applicable Barber Ji refund rules.

2. Customer Cancellation

Customers may cancel an eligible booking through the Barber Ji application, subject to the cancellation rules applicable to that booking.

Where a customer cancellation qualifies for a refund, the refundable amount may be determined according to the applicable refund settings and booking conditions.

A refund may be full or partial depending on the applicable refund rules.

3. Salon / Service Provider Cancellation

If a salon or service provider cancels or rejects an eligible booking, the booking may qualify for a refund according to the applicable Barber Ji refund rules.

The final refundable amount is determined after checking the booking and payment information.

4. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility is checked using the booking information and the applicable refund rules.

Depending on the booking, eligibility may consider:

  • Booking cancellation or rejection status
  • Who cancelled the booking
  • Payment method used for the booking
  • Whether customer cancellation is eligible
  • Whether salon/service-provider cancellation is eligible
  • Applicable refund time limits
  • Whether a full or partial refund rule applies

5. Full and Partial Refunds

Depending on the applicable refund rules, an eligible booking may receive either a full refund or a partial refund.

For a partial refund, the refundable amount is calculated according to the applicable refund configuration for the booking.

The refundable amount shown or determined for a booking is subject to the applicable refund rules and payment records.

6. Online Payments

For eligible bookings paid online, the refund may be processed using the applicable supported refund method.

Depending on the available refund option, customers may be asked to provide or select appropriate refund details such as:

  • Original payment method
  • UPI details
  • Bank account details

Payment processing is handled through the applicable payment infrastructure and financial institutions. The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may therefore depend on the payment provider or bank.

7. Cash / Pay at Salon Bookings

Cash or Pay at Salon bookings are handled differently from online payments.

Where a cash booking is eligible for a refund, the applicable refund process and available refund method will depend on the booking details and Barber Ji refund rules.

Customers should follow the refund instructions shown in the Barber Ji application for the relevant booking.

8. How to Request a Refund

For an eligible booking, customers can submit a refund request through the Barber Ji application.

The refund request may require:

  • Booking information
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Preferred supported refund method
  • Required UPI or bank information, where applicable

Refund requests are reviewed using the booking and payment information before processing.

9. Refund Processing

A submitted refund request may pass through different processing stages before the refund is completed.

The status of a refund request may include stages such as pending review, processing, refunded or rejected.

The actual time required to complete a refund can vary depending on verification, payment method, payment provider and banking processes.

10. Refund Request Review

Submitting a refund request does not by itself guarantee that a refund will be issued.

Barber Ji may review the booking status, cancellation details, payment information and applicable refund rules before approving a refund.

If a booking does not satisfy the applicable refund conditions, the refund request may be rejected.

11. Important Information

  • Refund eligibility is determined on the basis of the applicable booking and refund rules.
  • Refund amounts may differ between bookings.
  • Processing time may vary depending on the payment method and financial institution.
  • Customers should provide accurate refund information when submitting a refund request.
  • Barber Ji may update its refund and cancellation rules when required.

12. Policy Changes

Barber Ji may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time.

Any updated version will be published on this website with a revised "Last Updated" date.
