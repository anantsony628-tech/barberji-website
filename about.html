Welcome to Barber Ji

Barber Ji is a beauty and grooming service platform designed to connect customers with local salons, beauty parlours and professional service providers.

Our goal is to make discovering grooming and beauty services simple, convenient and accessible through a digital booking experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a convenient platform where customers can discover suitable beauty and grooming services and connect with local service providers.

Barber Ji aims to bring customers and salons together through an easy-to-use digital experience.

What We Offer

💈 Gents Salon Discover grooming and salon services for men.
💄 Ladies Parlour Explore beauty and personal care services for women.
✨ Unisex Salon Find salons offering services for different customer needs.
🏠 Home Service Access available beauty and grooming services at home.

For Customers

Customers can use Barber Ji to discover available salons and services, view service information and make bookings through the Barber Ji platform.

Depending on the service and availability, customers may have access to different payment and service options.

For Salon & Service Partners

Barber Ji also provides a platform for eligible salons and service providers to participate as partners.

Partners can provide their salon or service information and, subject to the applicable approval process, offer their services to customers through the platform.

Why Barber Ji?

  • Simple way to discover beauty and grooming services
  • Connection between customers and local service providers
  • Digital booking experience
  • Multiple salon and service categories
  • Support for eligible home-service offerings

Our Approach

Barber Ji focuses on providing a straightforward digital experience while allowing customers and service providers to interact through the platform.

Service availability, pricing, booking conditions and other details may vary by salon, service provider and service.
